About

I completed my Master's degree (MPhys) in 2022 at the University of Surrey studying Physics with Astronomy and achieving a First Class Honours. The work towards my Master's dissertation was completed at Radboud Universiteit in Nijmegen, the Netherlands, under the supervision of Dr. Nadedja Blagorodnova and Prof. Paul Groot. My dissertation was based on the development of a method that could be used to identify Galactic luminous red novae. The method made use of data from Gaia and time-domain surveys such as that from the Zwicky Transient Facility (see Research for more info). In October 2022 I started my Ph.D in Astrophysics at the University of Surrey under the supervision of Bob Nichol. My main focus is on analysising the spectra of type Ia supernovae with the aim of further constrianing cosmological parameters and more specifically the dark energy equation of state. I will be working with the Time Domain Extragalactic Survey (TiDES) collaboration, which is one of ten sub-surveys that will utilise the 4-metre Multi-Object Spectroscopic Telescope (4MOST) on the VISTA telescope. As part of TiDES I will develop codes to analyse spectra of the TiDES target supernovae events from LSST and other transient detection surveys. The analysis of the spectra will allow us to provide tranisent surveys with classifications that can improve the photometric classifications.
