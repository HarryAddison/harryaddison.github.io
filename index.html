About

I completed my Master's degree (MPhys) in 2022 at the University of Surrey studying Physics with Astronomy and achieving a First Class Honours. The work towards my Master's dissertation was completed at Radboud Universiteit in Nijmegen, the Netherlands, under the supervision of Dr. Nadedja Blagorodnova and Prof. Paul Groot. My dissertation was based on the development of a method that could be used to identify Galactic luminous red novae. The method made use of data from Gaia and time-domain surveys such as that from the Zwicky Transient Facility (see Research for more info). In 2022 I started my Ph.D at XXXXX researching XXXXX.
