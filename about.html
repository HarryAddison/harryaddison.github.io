Education

In 2018 I started at the University of Surrey, studing for a Masters in Physics with Astronomy. As part of my degree, in 2020 I conducted a research year at Radboud University, Nijmegen, the Netherlands, under the supervision of Prof. Nadejda Blagorodnova. My thesis research focused on developing methods to search archival data for luminous red novae precursors, with the aim of predicting future outbursts. In 2022 I successfully completed my Masters degree, obtaining a first-class degree.

Later in 2022, I started a PhD in astrophysics under the supervision of Prof. Robert (Bob) Nichol at the University of Surrey. My research foucs is supernovae, specifically with the upcoming spectroscopic survey TiDES. I am involved in designing and developing infrastructure for TiDES, including an automated spectral analysis pipeline and follow-up target selection
