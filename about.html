About Me

Education

Master's Degree

I started at the University of Surrey, studing for an integrated Masters in Physics with Astronomy. As part of the degree, in 2020 I conducted a research year at Radboud University, Nijmegen, the Netherlands, under the supervision of Prof. Nadejda Blagorodnova and Prof. Paul Groot. My thesis research focused on developing methods to search archival data for luminous red novae precursors, with the aim of predicting future outbursts. In 2022 I successfully completed my Masters degree, obtaining a first-class degree.

PhD

Research Focus

I stayed on at the University of Surrey to for PhD in Astrophysics under the supervision of Prof. Robert Nichol at the University of Surrey. My research foucs is supernovae, specifically with the upcoming spectroscopic survey TiDES. I am involved in designing and developing infrastructure for TiDES, including an automated spectral analysis pipeline and follow-up target selection.

Other Projects

Throughout my PhD, I have also been involved in 4MOST, working with the Science Communication (SciCom) working group and Commissioning Team. My involment in the SciCom working group involves writting news aticles that are published on the 4MOST news webpage, maintaining the news webpage content, and maintaining the content on 4MOST website gallery. With the Commissioning team, I have devopled codes and scripts that will be used to analyse the data taken during the Commissioning of 4MOST, analysed data taken in the lab, and will be involved in the taking commissioning observations with VISTA/4MOST, and analysing the commissioning data.

Hobbies and Interests

Music

At the age of 10 I was given the opportunity to learn an instrument of my choosing. Little me chose the French Horn due to intriging nature, not knowing its true difficulty! Throughout my school years, I took part in my local music service's brass and concert band ensembles. I was also very fortunate to be involved in many of the London Symphony Orchestra's (LSO) youth programmes. Highlights include performing under Gustavo Dudamell with the LA youth orchestra, performing at LSO's open air concerts at Trafalgar Square, and being apart of the Mayor's Jubilee brass band which performed on a music barge at the Queen's Diamond Jubilee river pageant in 2012.

Throughout my years at university I continued performing, although on a much smaller stage, being a valued member of the University of Surrey's concert band for 8 years, and the Univeristy of Surrey's orchestra for 4 years.

I continue to enjoy performing and being apart of ensembles.
